ANSWERS FOR FOUNDERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US resident own 100% of a US LLC?

Yes, absolutely. The United States allows foreign citizens of any nationality to own, manage, and operate a US Limited Liability Company (LLC) or C-Corporation with zero physical residency requirements, no US visa, and no SSN required.

Wyoming vs Delaware: Which one should I choose?

Choose Wyoming if you are a remote agency owner, software developer, e-commerce founder, or solopreneur seeking low upfront fees, low annual reports ($60), zero state tax, and maximum privacy. Choose Delaware only if you plan to raise institutional funding from US venture capital funds or angel investors who require Delaware jurisdiction.

Do I need to live in the state where my LLC is formed?

No. You can live anywhere in the world. VentNext provides your LLC with a commercial registered agent address in the state of formation to receive legal and government correspondence, keeping your home address 100% private.

Does my state charge income tax if I don't live in the US?

If your LLC has no US physical presence, no US employees, and no US "effectively connected income" (ECI), non-resident foreign owners typically owe 0% US state or federal income tax on active digital services or drop shipping businesses.
