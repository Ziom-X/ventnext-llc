Strategic Comparison for Non-Residents

Wyoming

#1 For Privacy & Remote Solopreneurs

Wyoming is the birthplace of the LLC. Member names remain strictly confidential from public records. Zero state corporate or personal income tax.

Filing Fee: $102
Annual Report: $60/year
Anonymity: 100% High

New Mexico

Lowest Cost & True Anonymous

The most economical state in the US. There is no annual report requirement and $0 annual state compliance fee, making ongoing upkeep minimal.

Filing Fee: $50
Annual Report: $0/year
Anonymity: 100% High

Delaware

Best for Tech Startups & VC Funding

Preferred by venture capital funds and angel investors due to its Court of Chancery, which resolves corporate disputes without juries using specialized corporate judges.

Filing Fee: $90
Franchise Tax: $300/year
Investor Appeal: Highest

Florida

Leading for E-commerce & Logistics

Ideal for founders planning physical trade, warehousing, Amazon FBA inventory, or connections to Latin American and Atlantic commercial routes.

Filing Fee: $125
Annual Report: $138.75/year
Commercial Hub: Booming
