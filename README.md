# VentNext - Formation & Federal Tax Services

A modern, high-converting web platform built for **VentNext** to offer official US corporate legal formation and federal tax infrastructure for international founders and non-residents.

---

## 🚀 Included Services (Mapped from HynoGo Structure)

1. **USA LLC Formation ($0 Service Fee Tier)**
   - Supported States: Wyoming ($102 state fee), New Mexico ($50 state fee), Delaware ($90 state fee), Florida ($125 state fee), and all 50 US states.
   - Includes: Articles of Organization, Operating Agreement, Banking Resolution, and Free Name Check.

2. **IRS 2-Day Fast EIN Service**
   - Official IRS CP-575 Federal Tax ID confirmation letter.
   - 100% procured without SSN or ITIN in 48-72 business hours.

3. **ITIN Application (Form W-7)**
   - IRS Certified Acceptance Agent (CAA) documentation.
   - Digital passport verification without mailing physical passports overseas.

4. **FinCEN BOI Compliance Filing ($35 Flat Rate)**
   - Corporate Transparency Act mandatory reporting.
   - Prevents $500/day federal penalties.

5. **Statutory Registered Agent & Commercial Address**
   - Official commercial physical address in Wyoming, Delaware, New Mexico, or Florida.
   - Same-day digital mail OCR scanning and secure portal delivery.

6. **Total Global Founder Suite (All-In-One Bundle)**
   - Full bundle with 1-on-1 concierge, US bank assistance (Mercury / Relay), and Stripe account guidance.
