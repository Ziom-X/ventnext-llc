Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about US company formation for non-residents.

Do I need to be a US citizen to work with VentNext?

No! Over 90% of our founders are non-US residents from over 70 countries. You do not need a US Social Security Number (SSN), ITIN, or US visa to legally own a 100% foreign-owned US LLC.

What information do you need from me to get started?

We don’t need any physical paperwork upfront. Just your desired company name, personal residential address, and contact details. Later, to open your Mercury/Relay business bank account, you’ll just need a clear international passport copy.

How does the 2-Day Fast EIN Service work without SSN?

Normally, the IRS fax queue takes 45 business days. Through VentNext's expedited federal tax team, we coordinate directly with the IRS to deliver your official CP-575 confirmation letter in as little as 48 to 72 hours.
